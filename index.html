The mission

Nobody talked about what it does to a child.
I'm talking about it now.

Through Taylor's Tears exists for the children who counted the nights, for the grandparents who weren't supposed to start over, for the aunts and neighbors and teachers who showed up when no one else did.

This is a space built on the belief that every wound — including the ones nobody sees — deserves to be named. That the silence that was demanded of us does not have to last forever.

You belong here.

Resources

If you or someone you love is struggling, help is available right now.

  • SAMHSA National Helpline Free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral and information for individuals and families. 1-800-662-4357
  • Adult Children of Alcoholics & Dysfunctional Families A 12-step fellowship for adults who grew up in alcoholic or dysfunctional homes. adultchildren.org
  • Al-Anon Family Groups Support for families and friends of people with a drinking or drug problem. al-anon.org
  • ACE Score — CDC Learn about Adverse Childhood Experiences and how they may have shaped you. Knowledge is the beginning of healing. cdc.gov/aces
