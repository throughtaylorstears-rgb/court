Her Story

She Was Built
in the Fire

Taylor's story doesn't start with a highlight reel. It starts in the dark — in the grip of addiction, in hospital waiting rooms, in courtrooms, in grief so heavy it felt impossible to breathe.

She watched friends overdose. She lost babies she never got to hold. She wore handcuffs and a jumpsuit. She sat in rooms full of people who had written her off. And through every single one of those moments — she kept choosing to get back up.

This brand is what happens when you refuse to let your pain be the last chapter. When you look at every wound and say: "That made me, it didn't break me."

Meet the World

The Faces of Through Taylor's Tears

Every character in this world carries a piece of a real story. They were built to show kids and families that hard things can be survived — and that love, even complicated love, is always worth fighting for.

Sam

The Mommy  ·  The Alter Ego  ·  The Overcomer

A loving mother fighting her way back from addiction while learning how to rebuild trust, stability, and her sense of self. Sam is the heart of this whole story — she is messy, determined, real, and unafraid to keep trying no matter how many times she falls.

Recovery Resilience Mother Fighter

The Little Girl

The Innocent  ·  The Hope  ·  The Why

She is the reason Sam keeps fighting. Bright-eyed, sweet in her yellow sundress, she represents every child waiting for a parent to come home — and every version of ourselves that deserved a softer world. She is the future Sam is building.

Hope Innocence Love Future

Blue Bunny

The Companion  ·  The Feeler  ·  The Truth-Teller

Blue Bunny says what everyone is feeling but nobody wants to say out loud. Hoppy. Sad. Confused. Not feeling fuzzy. Not hare for this today. He gives children — and adults — permission to name their emotions without shame. Sometimes you're just a little blue, and that's okay.

Emotions Mental Health Honesty Humor

The Mission

"I don't want to just survive my story. I want to use it — to show someone else still in the dark that there is a way through."

— Taylor

Through Taylor's Tears exists to break the silence around addiction, grief, incarceration, and the million forms of pain that women — especially mothers — carry alone. Through storytelling, characters, community, and radical honesty, this brand creates space for healing to happen out loud.

01

Break the Silence

Speak what gets kept in the dark — addiction, loss, grief, incarceration — with honesty that frees people.

02

Build Community

Create a space where women who have been through it can find each other, support each other, and rise together.

03

Turn Pain Into Purpose

Take every wound and transform it into something that helps the next person still fighting their way through.

The Lifestyle

B.O.S.S. Life
It's a Way of Living

B.O.S.S. Life isn't a title you earn — it's a decision you make every single day to show up for yourself and your people, even when it's hard. Especially when it's hard.

It's the energy Taylor carries. It's what she learned in the fire. You don't have to have it all together to be a boss — you just have to keep moving.
