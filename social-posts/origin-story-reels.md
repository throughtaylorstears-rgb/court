# Through Taylor's Tears — Origin Story Reels (4-Week Arc)

4 video reels told in first-person from the perspective of a 3-year-old girl who loves her mommy. AI-generated backgrounds, child's voiceover text overlay, warm emotional style.

## Suggested Posting Order

| Week | Post | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Reel 1 — Right Back | Hook. Establish the voice. Maximum emotional impact. |
| 2 | Reel 2 — I Waited | Escalate the abandonment theme. |
| 3 | Reel 3 — Grandma Came With A Bag | Introduce kinship care / separation. |
| 4 | Reel 4 — I Drew You A Picture | Close the arc with love, not anger. End on hope. |

## Production Notes

- **Format:** TikTok/Instagram Reel (9:16 vertical, 1080×1920)
- **Style:** AI-generated painterly backgrounds, warm and soft
- **Text:** White serif on semi-transparent dark overlay
- **Voiceover:** Record in a gentle, quiet voice — do NOT impersonate a child's voice; speak as an adult remembering
- **Music:** Soft, minimal piano or ambient — nothing distracting
- **Length target:** 30–45 seconds per reel
- **Recurring element:** Blue stuffed bunny appears as a constant across all reels — it represents the child's comfort object and continuity
