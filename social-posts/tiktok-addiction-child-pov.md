# TikTok: Addiction from a Child's POV
**Format:** Voiceover + on-screen text (slow scroll) | Quiet ambient sound or trending sad audio  
**Duration:** 45–60 seconds  
**Hook style:** Starts mid-thought — no intro, no greeting

---

## SCRIPT

> *(Text appears on screen, one line at a time. Child's voice, calm, matter-of-fact — that's what makes it devastating.)*

---

**[0:00 – 0:05 | HOOK]**

*"I used to hide the empty bottles before my friends came over."*

---

**[0:05 – 0:15]**

*"I was eight.*

*I didn't know why I was embarrassed.*

*I just knew that other kids' houses didn't smell like that."*

---

**[0:15 – 0:28]**

*"I learned to read moods like weather.*

*Morning quiet meant it might be a good day.*

*Ice in a glass at noon meant it wasn't.*

*I got really good at staying in my room."*

---

**[0:28 – 0:40]**

*"The hardest part wasn't the chaos.*

*It was the moments in between —*

*when they'd laugh at something I said,*

*and for a second it felt completely normal.*

*I'd hold onto those like they were proof."*

---

**[0:40 – 0:52]**

*"Nobody told me I was living inside someone else's addiction.*

*I just thought I wasn't enough*

*to make them stop."*

---

**[0:52 – 1:00 | CLOSE]**

*"If you grew up like this —*

*you were enough.*

*You always were."*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Audio:** "Death Bed" by Powfu / any soft lo-fi trending sound, OR record in complete silence — silence hits harder
- **Text font:** Clean serif or handwritten, white on dark background, no animations — just slow fade
- **No face on camera** needed; text-only performs well for this topic and protects privacy
- **CTA in comments** (pin this): "Resources for children of people with addiction: [link]"
- **Hook test:** The first line should stop the scroll. "I used to hide the empty bottles" is specific and visual — it works.
